ABUSEMENT PARK

When the admins abandoned their gc "Survivors of the Burnt School," the core crew refused to let the bond die. In April 2024, Dhaya, Ace, Niko, Wajiha, Rubaina, Dhanya, Divi, Manav, and Raj officially established Abusement Park—turning a lost community into the most legendary, chaotic chat on Instagram.

💡

THE NAME

It earned its name for a reason: "Abusement Park" was a non-stop rollercoaster of drama, fights, and pure chaos. Yet, against all odds, it remains legendary—easily one of the greatest group chats anyone has ever joined.

📈

THE TIMELINE

Spanning eight generations, Abusement Park is a revolving door of absolute madness. Since April 2024, hundreds have joined and left, but the core veterans always stayed to keep the torch burning. Each wave brought fresh energy, reshaping the chat while keeping its chaotic spirit alive. The daily rhythm never changes: total silence all day, a barrage of reels by evening, and a 2 AM peak of either deep talks or World War III. That exact unpredictability is why this chat refuses to die.

💕

OUR GC

People come and go, but the foundation stays unbothered. Despite a non-stop cycle of arguments, misunderstandings, and clashes, the loyalty among the OGs Dhaya, Ace, Avi, Ema, Niko, Yahiya, Manav and the elder members like Akshaya, Ayesha, Momo, Aditya, Roshan, Ankita, Sandhya, Aryan is set in stone. We fight, but we hold it down harder. No matter how toxic the timeline gets, this crew is the reason the park never shuts down.
