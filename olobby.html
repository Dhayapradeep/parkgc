HOW TO PLAY

1

Two players battle on an 8 × 8 board.

2

Black moves first, followed by White.

3

Place your disc so that one or more opponent discs are trapped between your discs.

4

Every trapped opponent disc is flipped to your color.

5

If you have no legal move, you must pass your turn.

6

When the game ends, the player with the most discs wins and receives Chaos Points.
