HOW TO PLAY

1

Two players enter the room. Black moves first.

2

Place your piece beside your opponent's pieces to surround them.

3

Surrounded opponent pieces are flipped to your colour.

4

Players take turns placing one piece on every valid move.

5

If you have no legal move, your turn is automatically skipped.

6

When neither player can move, the player with the most pieces wins.
